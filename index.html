# About Me

Hello! I'm a passionate Software Developer and working student currently pursuing a degree in Information Technologies (via the BrightStart program at IPS). My academic background is built on a solid foundation, starting with a professional certification as an Information Systems Programming Technician from ATEC, and now advancing through my bachelor's degree.


I am a creator at heart. Whenever I encounter a problem or see an opportunity to improve a daily task, my first instinct is to build a program to solve it. My technical interests are broad and dynamic—I enjoy web development and building various software projects using languages like C#, Python, and JavaScript. Alongside standard software, I also have a strong passion for developing games.


When I'm not writing code, you can usually find me playing video games or watching movies and TV series. For me, these aren't just ways to pass the time; they are a constant source of inspiration that fuels my imagination and helps me brainstorm mechanics and concepts for new projects.


Right now, my primary focus is excelling in my degree while consistently expanding my portfolio. Due to my academic commitments, I am not looking for full-time roles, but I am fully open to freelance work and short-term projects. Looking ahead, my goal is to keep writing clean code, developing useful software, and bringing my own game ideas to life.
